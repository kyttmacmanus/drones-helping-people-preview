AN INTERACTIVE DATA SAMPLE

Interactive Lamont surface reconstruction.

Drag to rotate. Arrow keys also rotate; + / − zoom. Points are a deterministic sample from 603 source LAS tiles.

Lamont point-cloud sample

The sample represents reconstructed surface geometry from the Lamont photogrammetric archive. It illustrates a spatial-data input relevant to EFPL’s terrain and wind-dynamics research.

The viewer displays sampled photogrammetric surface points. RGB values originate in the source files; the alternative color scale represents height relative to the sample’s minimum Z. Independent positional accuracy and bare-earth classification are not established by this viewer.
