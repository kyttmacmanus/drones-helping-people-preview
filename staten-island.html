STATEN ISLAND / STUDENTS & COMMUNITY PARTNERS

Fieldwork along
the North Shore.

The Staten Island record connects course-based instruction, a community partnership, and drone mapping of waterfront sites. Working-group photographs and survey products document different parts of that work.

01 / THE PROJECT CONTEXT

SDEV4650 and
community-engaged mapping.

2023 course fieldwork

A December 2023 course account describes a completed Staten Island drone operation and a 3D model of a historic firehouse. Collaboratory support included a drone purchase for SDEV4650. CIESIN, the teaching team, students, community partners, and city officials contributed to the work.

December 2025 North Shore mapping

Flights at North Shore Esplanade on December 1, 2025 produced imagery and elevation products. EFPL’s work continued the Staten Island community partnership, with products collected for examining waterfront conditions and resilience questions.

January 2024 follow-up correspondence records technical support for making the student StoryMap and map layers shareable with the Staten Island Urban Center (SIUC). This extends the record from field collection to work on partner access; final delivery and subsequent use are not established by that exchange.

The project record establishes field activities and spatial outputs. Evidence of use in planning or community outcomes should be assessed separately with partners.
