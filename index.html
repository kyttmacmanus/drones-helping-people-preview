2017–PRESENT · ONGOING & FORTHCOMING

Program timeline

Selected milestones connect field instruction, community relationships, spatial products and continuing research.

  1. 2017

    October 28, 2017

    Balloon mapping and field GIS

    Three Columbia GIS courses combined balloon aerial imagery with ground observations at Black Rock Forest.

    Teaching & Workshops
    Context and project

    Public Lab balloon systems and visible/modified-infrared cameras preceded the later drone-teaching program. High winds limited the balloon operation.

    Black Rock Forest balloon mapping →

  2. 2021

    2021

    Teaching capability

    Provost-supported teaching capability

    The Emerging Technology Grant supported drone and visualization equipment for GIS teaching.

    Teaching & Workshops
    Context and project

    The grant supported Drones and AR/VR for 3D Modeling in GIS. Later course fieldwork connected that capability with practical observation and analysis.

    Callicoon student fieldwork →

  3. 2022

    April 4–5, 2022

    Callicoon field exercise

    SDEV3450 students collected aerial observations and developed student spatial analyses during a two-day field exercise.

    Teaching & Workshops
    Context and project

    Fieldwork connected vegetation, terrain and other geospatial questions with jointly produced student mapping. Layout detail is not independent accuracy.

    Callicoon student fieldwork →

  4. 2023

    December 2, 2023

    Staten Island community mapping

    SDEV4650 students and community partners worked around 60 Hannah Street with drone observations and structural reconstruction.

    Communities
    Context and project

    Collaboratory funding included a teaching drone purchase. Follow-up work supported partner access to the student StoryMap and map layers through SIUC.

    60 Hannah Street community fieldwork →

  5. 2025

    March 11, 2025

    Campus tree-mapping workshop

    Nicole Davi hosted a William Paterson workshop connecting Field Maps tree observations with drone-derived products.

    Teaching & Workshops
    Context and project

    Visible/thermal camera observations and later elevation products supplied complementary views. Tree-identification performance was not evaluated by these displays.

    William Paterson tree mapping →

  6. 2025

    April–May 2025

    NYC Parks UAS research begins

    EFPL and Natan Charytan began permitted Four Sparrow Marsh fieldwork and repeat mapping.

    NYC Parks
    Context and project

    Kytt's first-hand account identifies April 18 as the first UAS operation approved by NYC Parks. Wind curtailed that operation; the team returned May 2. The photograph records the May visit.

    Repeated mapping at Four Sparrow Marsh →

  7. 2025

    December 2025

    North Shore and Morningside mapping

    December fieldwork extended the Parks portfolio and continued the Staten Island community partnership.

    CommunitiesNYC Parks
    Context and project

    North Shore Esplanade was mapped December 1 and Morningside Park December 18. These are separate site activities with their own source dates and products.

    North Shore Esplanade mapping →Morningside Park mapping and research →

    April 15, 2026

    Ground and aerial field methods

    SDEV3450 and WAC partners connected control/positioning observations with photogrammetry, LiDAR and complementary instruments.

    Teaching & Workshops
    Context and project

    Hemlock Hill field instrumentation →

    June 2026

    Field collaboration and working sessions shared acquisition, photogrammetric processing and interpretation methods.

    June 13, 2026

    Selected-site mapping around Mtukuzi

    Local collaborators and UNZA field colleagues documented village, school, agricultural, river and water-well sites.
