Classroom preparation, equipment setup, flight observation, and spatial analysis form different parts of the teaching record. This collection brings the workshop photographs and related spatial products into one view.

01 / THE TEACHING CONTEXT

Equipment support,
coursework and workshops.

The classroom foundation

The 2021 Provost Emerging Technology Grant supported Drones and AR/VR for 3D Modeling in GIS, with $19,637 and CUIT support. It established equipment and software capacity for instruction.

Course-based fieldwork

SDEV3450 and SDEV4650 activities connected drone operations, spatial analysis, and field-data collection. Collaboratory funding for SDEV4650 included a drone purchase.

Research instrumentation

At EFPL, DURIP support for the IF1200A enabled an additional research capability associated with ARO work. That acquisition is distinct from the teaching awards.

Workshop participation and equipment use are documented activities. Learning outcomes require learner assessment; the photographs alone do not establish those outcomes.

Earlier GIS education with Earth2Class

The Earth2Class archive lists Kytt MacManus’s GIS education workshops with Steve Kluge on April 18, 2009 and Malanding Jaiteh on September 26, 2009. These are earlier educator workshops in geographic information and visualization; they are kept outside the drone-activity chronology. Earth2Class workshop archive ↗

Source photographs

Drone camera frames document the field of view at capture time. They are kept distinct from group photographs and from geometrically processed mapping products.

Derived products

Orthomosaics and photogrammetric reconstructions show outputs of image processing. The methods page describes resolution, coordinate systems, processing-run identity, and accuracy limits.
