02 / FROM IMAGE TO PRODUCT

Product definitions
and interpretation.

01

Source images

Individual camera records show visible conditions from a particular time and viewpoint. Keep original files and collection context together.

02

Orthomosaics

Processed imagery provides a map-like view of selected coverage. Examine edges, gaps, alignment, source resolution, and the processing record.

03

Terrain & surface models

A DSM represents the visible surface, including vegetation and structures. A DTM is a processed terrain interpretation; its label alone does not verify bare-earth accuracy.

04

Point clouds & meshes

Photogrammetric points and textured surfaces make scene structure visible. They are not automatically LiDAR, independently controlled elevations, or evidence of wind or water movement.

Terms used in the atlas
TermMeaning in this site
OrthomosaicA georeferenced image composite produced from overlapping photographs after geometric processing.
GSDGround sampling distance: the ground dimension represented by an image pixel. Capture-report GSD and output raster pixel spacing describe resolution, not independently tested accuracy.
DSM / DTMDigital surface model / digital terrain model. A DSM represents visible surfaces; a DTM is a processed estimate of terrain with surface objects removed or classified.
GCP / checkpointA ground-control point supplies surveyed control to processing. An independent checkpoint is reserved for evaluating positional accuracy.
Point cloud / LASA set of three-dimensional points; LAS is a standard point-cloud file format. A LAS file may contain photogrammetric points and does not by itself indicate LiDAR acquisition.
Textured meshA reconstructed surface model with imagery applied as texture. Rendered views describe scene structure and appearance.

Processing-run identity

The combined Mtukuzi run referenced in Raphael’s lesson recorded 515 of 979 images calibrated and average camera GSD of 0.016 m. Its report explicitly states that GCPs and independent checkpoints were not used. Those metrics do not apply automatically to the five separate companion missions in this atlas.

FOUR SPARROW / 2025

Four Sparrow survey chronology.

April 18 was the first NYC Parks-approved UAS operation, confirmed by Kytt MacManus; wind curtailed it. The May 2 survey produced an orthomosaic of the mapped footprint. Local capture archives also document August 7 and December 5. An earlier results slide labels a repeat survey August 27; that discrepancy remains open.

The May, August, and December capture exports repeat identical headline statistics. This atlas preserves them as reported records, without treating repeated values as independent change measurements.

NYC PARKS / DECEMBER 2025

December 2025 mapped products.

North Shore Esplanade layouts are labeled December 1, 2025. Morningside layouts are labeled December 18, 2025. Orthomosaics, surface and terrain models, and the Morningside 3D view now appear in the visual catalog. The catalog distinguishes these completed products from planning graphics.

LAMONT / PHOTOGRAMMETRIC ARCHIVE

Lamont source-tile reconstruction.

The interactive Lamont view samples 603 source LAS tiles containing 16,541,307 points. Each tile was read with its own coordinate scale and offset. The roughly 601 × 676 m extent is a source-data overview, with no independent accuracy claim.

The root merged LAS file was excluded: its coordinates occupy a roughly 31 m cube, inconsistent with the source-tile coverage. The viewer uses the source tiles. The processing report describes photogrammetry; this is not a LiDAR or bare-earth model. Snowy field photographs have February 19, 2024 EXIF dates even though a parent folder name suggests 2025.
